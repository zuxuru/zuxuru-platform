# Fukulisane Vision & Architecture

> The complete blueprint for Fukulisane — the AI-powered Business Growth Operating System.

## The Journey

### 1. Discover
Fukulisane scans what already exists: Facebook, TikTok, business name, existing content, reviews, competitors, local search presence.

Produces: **Current Visibility Score: 31/100**

### 2. Build Digital Infrastructure
Identifies gaps, helps create: Website, Google Business Profile, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, local directory presence, email, lead forms, SEO, brand identity.

### 3. AI Brand Ambassador
Creates a digital brand personality — consistent appearance, personality, voice, expertise. Positioned as **AI Brand Ambassador / Virtual Influencer**, not a fake human.

### 4. Strategic Content
Instead of "create 10 posts", the agent thinks: "What content generates qualified customers this week?" Categories: Attention → Trust → Conversion → Retention.

### 5. AI Closer
Lead capture → AI qualification (Hot/Warm/Cold) → AI follows up → hands to business or approved sales workflow.

## The Loop

```
SCAN → SCORE → DIAGNOSE → PLAN → EXECUTE → PUBLISH → GENERATE LEADS → CONVERT → MEASURE → RESCORE
```

## Architecture

- **Analyzer** — Visibility, competitors, SEO, leads
- **Creator** — Content, images, video, copy
- **Executor** — Platform APIs, website, CRM, publishing
- **Sales Agent** — Leads → Customers

## Key Principles

1. Sell **AI Business Growth**, not AI content
2. AI decides WHAT happens, Fukulisane decides WHAT IS ALLOWED
3. Three approval levels: 🟢 Automatic, 🟡 Approval required, 🔴 Explicit authorization
4. Modular connector architecture
5. Business Digital Graph — platforms aren't independent, they're connected
6. Score becomes the ecosystem control panel
7. Business Agent goal: "I want 30 new customers this month"
